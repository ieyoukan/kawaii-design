# References

Real sites worth studying. Learn the techniques, never copy the layout, art, logo or type.

## a monotone portfolio site — character-colored monotone portfolio



- The whole palette comes from the character: blue-gray, lavender and a little cream. No warm background "for cuteness".
- No borders anywhere. A lighter rounded panel sits on a slightly darker page, and that is the only framing.
- The top page has a chibi character on a circular mat in a pale tint of its own color.
- On the about page, the character breaks out of the hero panel and is cut off by its bottom edge. A sticker-style offset shadow in the character's dark tint gives it weight.
- The hero background is a faint dashed grid, a quiet check pattern.
- The custom display type does the decorating: chunky rounded letters with dots in the counters. The body text is plain and quiet.
- Shape language: circles and large rounded rectangles.

## an illustrator site — illustrator site, dreamy and illustration-led



- The first screen is a full-bleed illustration. The logo overlays a calm part of it. The menu is a small organic tab in the corner.
- Section transitions use a layered wave (`.wave` + `.wave.back`). Two copies of a hand-drawn wave strip sit on the next section's top edge: the front one is opaque, and the back one is mirrored at 40% opacity. They drift sideways at 40s and 60s. See "Layered wave edge" in [shapes-and-patterns.md](shapes-and-patterns.md).
- Background: a fixed pastel wash with a faint geometric line pattern (squares, circles, waves) on top.
- Section titles are a giant faint ghost word behind the section.
- Works hang on strings with binder clips, on white translucent cards without borders.
- The footer is calm: a wave edge into white, then the navigation in the display face, the SNS icons and a copyright line. No paragraphs, no buttons.
- Shape language: organic for the world, rounded rectangles for the works, circles for buttons.

## a pastel campaign site — airy, slightly grown-up pastel



- One `linear-gradient(168deg, …)` covers the whole page container, about 7,000px tall: pale pink → white → aqua → blue-white → lavender-white → pink → mint. Because it stretches over the full page, each screen shows only a slight shift. It reads as 透明感, not as a gradient.
- On top of it, flat illustrations with soft cast shadows, set in a simple 3D space (a floor plane and an arch).
- Light gray type, generous letter spacing and vertical rhythm. Grown-up rather than childish.

## What they share

- The illustration is huge and clearly the star.
- The surfaces are white or a pale tint of the star's own colors.
- They don't use outlines, sticker buttons or warm cream by default.
- The cuteness lives in display type, one shape language and faint patterns, not in UI decoration.
