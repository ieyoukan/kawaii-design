# Kawaii dialects

Kawaii is not one style. Pick one dialect per product and stay in it. Mixing two (for example ゆめかわ waves with レトロ stamps) reads as random.

Palettes are starting points. Always tune them to the star's colors in Star mode.

## ゆるかわ (yuru-kawa): relaxed and loose

- **Feel**: のんびり, slightly clumsy, hand-drawn, no pressure.
- **Good for**: diaries, habit trackers, community and hobby apps, small shops, personal sites.
- **Palette**: off-white paper `#FBF7F0`, warm ink `#4A3F37`, muted accents like sage `#A8BFA0`, apricot `#F2B48C`, sky `#A9C8E0`.
- **Shape**: medium radius, slightly irregular hand-drawn dividers or underlines, soft 1.5px ink outlines.
- **Type**: Zen Maru Gothic or Kiwi Maru for headings, a rounded gothic for body. Klee One or Yomogi only for one accent.
- **Motif**: a small doodle, a single plant or animal that recurs.
- **Copy**: plain, a bit slow. 「きょうもおつかれさまでした」
- **Pitfall**: too many wobbly hand-drawn lines makes it look messy rather than relaxed.

## ゆめかわ (yume-kawa): dreamy

- **Feel**: floaty, iridescent, dreamlike, a little unreal. Like a soap bubble or holographic marble.
- **Good for**: SNS and community apps, music, VTuber and idol fan pages, fashion, cosmetics, events, anything asked for as 「ゆめかわいい」.
- **Palette**: led by the character's main color. An aqua character gets aqua as the main hue (50 to 70% of the color), with pink and lavender as partners and a touch of lemon or mint. A pink character gets pink as the main hue, a purple one lavender. White sits between the colors everywhere, and a deep ink in the main hue's family is used for text. Every surface, accent, icon and illustration takes its color from this set.
- **Background**: never flat and never a plain wallpaper pattern. Use an iridescent gradient: radial pools from several points mixed with soft linear bands, distorted so they swirl organically, with white light between the colors and the character's main hue leading. See "ゆめかわ background" in [backgrounds-and-images.md](backgrounds-and-images.md). Tiny white sparkle specks can sit on top, sparsely.
- **Surfaces: glass.** Panels, cards, the nav and inputs are frosted glass: translucent white, blurred background behind, a thin bright edge and a soft colored shadow. The background's colors glow through the UI, which is what makes it feel dreamy. See "Glass UI" in [backgrounds-and-images.md](backgrounds-and-images.md).
- **Shape**: large radius on glass panels, pill buttons, circles for avatars and bubbles. Organic wave edges if sections change.
- **Accents**: iridescent bubbles (a circle with a pink-to-aqua rim and a white highlight), small stars or sparkles. Use them as accents in a few places, not as wallpaper.
- **Type**: rounded geometric display for the logo and titles, wide letter spacing, a clean rounded gothic for body. Text stays solid deep plum, never gradient.
- **Motion**: choose a single soft entrance or one ambient movement, such as a drifting bubble. See [Motion](tokens.md#motion).
- **Reference**: an illustrator site, see [refs.md](refs.md).
- **Pitfall**: readability. Glass over a busy gradient can wash text out. Give text panels enough white (55 to 75% opacity) and keep the ink deep. Check contrast on the most colorful part of the background.

## ワントーン (one-tone): the character's color, everywhere

- **Feel**: coherent, calm, quietly devoted to one character.
- **Good for**: personal sites of creators and VTubers, single-character pages, portfolios with a signature avatar.
- **Palette**: built entirely from one character's color family. For a blue-gray character, use a page `#DCE0E8`, a panel `#EEF1F5`, ink `#4A4F6A` and an accent from the hair or eyes. No warm "cute" accent from outside the character.
- **Shape**: circles (a mat behind the character, round icons) and large rounded panels. No borders. Separate surfaces by value.
- **Layout**: the character is large and breaks out of its panel. Faint dashed grid or check in the hero.
- **Type**: a chunky, rounded, geometric display face for the name and titles. Plain gothic for body.
- **Reference**: a monotone portfolio site, see [refs.md](refs.md).
- **Pitfall**: with too little contrast between page, panel and ink it turns gray and sleepy. Keep the ink deep and the character's accent color visible.

## ポップかわいい (pop): candy and stickers

- **Feel**: energetic, bold, playful, like a sticker sheet.
- **Good for**: games, kids, events, snack and toy products, campaigns.
- **Palette**: saturated candy colors such as tomato `#FF6B5B`, lemon `#FFD84D`, soda `#4FC3E8` and grass `#6CCB6A` on cream `#FFF8EA`, with a thick dark ink `#2B2233`.
- **Shape**: thick 2 to 3px outlines, solid offset "sticker" shadows (`0 4px 0 ink`), chunky buttons that press down.
- **Type**: heavy rounded display (M PLUS Rounded 1c 800, Fredoka) for headings, regular rounded gothic for body.
- **Motif**: stickers, stars, speech bubbles.
- **Pitfall**: when every element has a thick outline and shadow, nothing stands out. Use the full sticker treatment on primary actions and the star only.

## レトロかわいい (retro / 昭和ファンシー): stationery shop nostalgia

- **Feel**: nostalgic, printed, a fancy-goods shop from decades ago.
- **Good for**: cafes, bakeries, zines, crafts, merch shops, personal sites.
- **Palette**: cream `#F6EEDC`, tomato red `#D9493B`, mustard `#E3A73B`, teal `#2F8C88`, brown ink `#3B2A20`.
- **Shape**: small to medium radius, ribbons and label shapes, dotted and scalloped borders, a light print texture.
- **Type**: rounded gothic plus a retro display face for one heading style.
- **Motif**: polka dots, gingham, stamps, price tags.
- **Pitfall**: too much texture and pattern behind content. Keep patterns to borders and empty bands.

## 大人かわいい (otona-kawaii): restrained and grown-up

- **Feel**: calm, refined, soft. Kawaii in a single detail, never childish.
- **Good for**: cosmetics, fashion, lifestyle, wellness, B2C services for adults, SaaS that wants warmth.
- **Palette**: dusty muted tones (くすみカラー) such as greige `#E9E3DC`, dusty rose `#C9A3A0`, smoke blue `#9FB1BC`, olive `#A3A57E`, with deep cocoa ink `#3A302C`.
- **Shape**: small to medium radius (8 to 14px on cards), thin lines, lots of space.
- **Type**: a refined gothic or a soft mincho for headings, gothic for body. Rounded faces only for small accents.
- **Motif**: one: a small ribbon, a dot, a tiny line-drawn icon.
- **Copy**: polite and quiet. No onomatopoeia except maybe one.
- **Pitfall**: going so quiet that it becomes generic minimal. Keep one charm point visible on every screen.
- **More**: see "大人かわいい in more detail" in [character.md](character.md).

## ぶさかわ / きもかわ (busa-kawa / kimo-kawa): odd but lovable

- **Feel**: awkward, weird, deadpan, lovable because it's off.
- **Good for**: only when the brand or character already has this personality.
- **Rule**: the character carries all the oddness. The UI stays plain and steady (neutral palette, simple shapes, dry copy) so the weirdness lands. A weird UI around a weird character cancels out.

## Choosing

| If the product is... | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| For adults, everyday use, needs trust | 大人かわいい |
| Personal, slow, journaling, community | ゆるかわ |
| Games, kids, events, high energy | ポップかわいい |
| Food, crafts, shops with a physical feel | レトロかわいい |
| Fan culture, music, dreamy IP | ゆめかわ |
| A creator or VTuber with one signature character | ワントーン |
| An established odd character | ぶさかわ / きもかわ |

If the star already has a strong style, the dialect follows the star, not the table.
